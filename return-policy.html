Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Return Window

You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery, as long as the item is unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags attached.

2. Non-Returnable Items

Items marked as final sale, and personal-care Women, cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

3. How to Request a Return

Go to "My Account" → "My Orders", find the relevant order, and contact us with your order number to start the return process.

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

5. Exchanges

If you'd like a different size or color, let us know when requesting your return and we'll arrange an exchange, subject to availability.

6. Damaged or Wrong Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos, and we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund.
